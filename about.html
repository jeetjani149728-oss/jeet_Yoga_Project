Our roots run back fourteen years.

RadiantRoots began as a single weekly class in a borrowed room. Today it's a full studio — but the reason for showing up hasn't changed.

Our Story

A sanctuary for peace, mindfulness and holistic wellness.

Welcome to RadiantRoots — your home for self-discovery and physical rejuvenation through the art of yoga. Established in 2010, we've spent over a decade helping people find balance amid busy lives, one breath at a time.

We offer classes for every level, from first-timers to advanced practitioners, in a studio built to feel less like a gym and more like a room a friend would invite you into.

What We Believe

Three ideas hold this studio together.

Breath first

Every pose in this studio starts with breath, not effort. Move at the pace your breath can keep.

Every body welcome

Modifications are offered, never assumed. There's no "advanced" version of belonging here.

Consistency over intensity

We'd rather see you once a week for a year than every day for a month. Small steps, kept.
